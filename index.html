Evolution Arena

Twenty paper bots trading US stocks and options in one pool — stock shapes (stk_: momentum, reversion, breakout, baskets…) and options shapes (opt_: reversion calls/puts, credit spreads, iron condors) compete for the same slots. Each week selection keeps the bots with the most profit (less half their worst drawdown) and breeds them into the losers, so evolution decides how much of the fleet trades shares vs. contracts.

How fitness works

Each bot is scored on a Sortino-style ratio over its continuous equity curve (cash + realized + unrealized) since its current genome was assigned — so a smooth climb beats a bumpy one with the same endpoint, and realizing a gain never reads as a drawdown. Two guards sink non-competitors below every real player: a bot that never traded (idle) or deploys under of the risk cap (low-deploy) can't fake-win by sitting in cash. Fitness is EMA-smoothed across generations so one lucky week can't flip elite status.

Everything here is current-strategy only. "P/L this strategy" is equity − base since the bot's current genome was assigned, and fitness is that same window's risk-adjusted path. Cumulative "since $100k" P/L is deliberately not shown — it's polluted by earlier genomes that were culled and no longer exist, so it can't evaluate the strategy running now. The window resets at each evolution, so right after one these numbers start near zero and build over the week — that week-over-week gain is the real score.
