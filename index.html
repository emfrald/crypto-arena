Evolution Arena

Twenty paper bots trading US stocks and options in one pool — stock shapes (stk_: momentum, reversion, breakout, baskets…) and options shapes (opt_: reversion calls/puts, credit spreads, iron condors) compete for the same slots. Each week selection keeps the bots with the most profit (less half their worst drawdown) and breeds them into the losers, so evolution decides how much of the fleet trades shares vs. contracts.

How fitness works

Each bot is ranked on a trailing window (about four weeks, see the snapshot) of its continuous equity curve: excess P/L over buy-and-hold, minus a drawdown penalty, minus a downside-volatility penalty. All three are divided by the same dollar cap, so a small smooth earner does not beat a large bumpy one. Sharpe and Sortino are shown when the path is long enough; they are not the ranking key. A bot with too few days or trades is warming up — it is not culled and it is not bred. A bot that never traded scores 0 (the same as cash), not a fake floor that made any small loser look like the leader. Low deployment (under of the cap) shrinks a positive score instead of sinking the bot below every real number.

"P/L this strategy" is life-of-genome (equity − base since this genome was assigned). Fitness uses only the trailing window of that same genome, so an old lucky month falls out of the ranking without erasing the P/L column. Cumulative "since $100k" P/L is not shown — it mixes in genomes that were already culled.
